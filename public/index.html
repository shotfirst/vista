      What the refute pass changed

      After the first research pass I tried to break each number. These are the ones that moved, and the ones I still can't prove.

      • The rodeo fills the city. The National Finals Rodeo runs 3 to 12 December at the Thomas and Mack Center, your whole window. On its first weekend, rooms average close to US$400 a night. Midweek and the second weekend run lower. I priced stays at rodeo rates, and the long trip leaves town on the busiest weekday nights.
      • The South Rim now costs Canadians extra. Since 1 January 2026, visitors from outside the US aged 16 and up pay US$100 each on top of the US$35 car fee. For the four adults that is US$435, or US$285 with the US$250 non resident pass, which covers four adults. Kai and Keo enter free. Zion has the same rule. Grand Canyon West, Hoover Dam, Red Rock and Valley of Fire do not.
      • Grand Canyon West kids-free deal only works at the window. Buy an adult pass in person and up to two kids aged 5 to 12 enter free. Booking online forfeits it. Keo is free either way. The site says it is not wheelchair accessible, and you park at the gate and ride shuttles between viewpoints.
      • Flights are recent prices, not quotes for your dates. Air Canada and Porter fly nonstop. Sale fares hit CA$188 to CA$291 return this year; Google Flights shows Air Canada from CA$351. Early December with a rodeo in town will sit higher, so I used CA$380 to CA$550 a seat, and more for the comfort plus tier. Keo is 3, so he needs his own seat.
      • December at the South Rim is real winter. Highs around 7°C, nights well below freezing, a few inches of snow most weeks. The Rim Trail is paved and flat, so Stephanie and Fei will be fine with warm layers. Hermit Road closes to cars from 1 December, which means less driving and more shuttle.
      • Still unproven. Rodeo week hotel rates for your exact nights, Kai's Vegas entry fee (Louisville was US$40, I assumed the same), the Polar Express dates in Williams, Discovery Children's Museum pricing, and every food figure. Each line below says sourced or estimate.
